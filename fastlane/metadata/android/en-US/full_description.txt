GT-BMS (GNUTUX BMS) reads smart lithium batteries straight from their battery management system over Bluetooth LE.

* Automatic protocol detection: Bestway BWBM, JBD/Xiaoxiang, PowerQueen/LiTime, 123SmartBMS, JK-BMS and Daly (experimental)
* Live data: state of charge, voltage, current, power, capacity, cycles, temperatures, MOS state, alerts
* Per-cell voltages with highest/lowest highlighted
* Device information, protection parameters and event history (Bestway)
* Full-charge and low-battery alarms
* Saved batteries and fast reconnect
* Arabic and English interface
* Read-only: never changes BMS settings
